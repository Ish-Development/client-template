# Logo

Fictional sample brand.

## Hard rules
```yaml
- rule: Do not stretch
  why: The wordmark's round letters are the brand's calm. Stretched, they look tense.
  where: Everywhere
- rule: Do not put on busy photos without a quiet field
  why: The logo is thin and disappears on texture.
  where: Photography, social, packaging shots
- rule: Do not recolor outside ink, paper, or sea
  why: Any other color reads as a different brand.
  where: Everywhere
```
