## By channel

Website
Lead with relief, then proof, then product.
Hero: one sentence + one action.
Do not stack three value props above the fold.

Product pages
1. What it is
2. What it is not
3. How to use it tonight
4. Materials / ingredients
5. Shipping / returns in plain English

Ads
One object. One benefit. No lifestyle manifesto.

Packaging
Few words. Large type. Instructions a tired person can follow.

Support
Assume good intent. Answer the question first. Then offer one extra step, not five.
