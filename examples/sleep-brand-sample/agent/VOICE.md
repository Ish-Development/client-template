## Diction

### Words we use
| Concept | We say | Instead of | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| sleep | sleep | recovery, sleep performance | The buyer wants to sleep, not to perform. |
| routine | evening routine | protocol, ritual | A routine is ordinary. Protocol sounds clinical, ritual sounds precious. |
| mask | eye mask | sleep accessory, slumber essential | Says what it is. Nobody searches for "slumber essential". |
| lamp | lamp | light therapy device | Therapy is a medical claim we do not make. |

### Product names
| Product | Name |
|---|---|
| Eye mask | CLIENT_NAME Mask |
| Lamp | CLIENT_NAME Lamp |
| Supplement | CLIENT_NAME Magnesium |

### Words we never use
- biohack: turns rest into another thing to get good at.
- optimize: same. Sleep is not a score.
- protocol: sounds clinical. We are not a clinic.
- stack: supplement-bro language. Wrong room.
- hack: promises a shortcut. There is none.
- journey: category cliché. Every wellness brand says it.
- disrupt: about us, not about the buyer.
- unlock: implies the buyer is locked. Shame by another name.
- ecosystem: product-speak. Buyers have a bedroom, not an ecosystem.

## Syntax and rhythm
Prefer short lines. One claim at a time. Let white space do work.

## We say
"Put the phone in another room."
"This is magnesium glycinate. It is the form that sits easiest for most people."
"If you are still awake, that is allowed."

## We do not say
"Unlock your best self."
"Optimize your circadian stack."
"Join the sleep revolution."
"Wellness journey."

## Do and don't, by channel

Website headline
Do: Sleep that does not ask more of you.
Don't: The future of restorative performance.

Product description
Do: A linen eye mask weighted just enough to stay put. No scent. No lights.
Don't: A luxurious slumber essential designed to elevate your nightly ritual.

Email subject
Do: A quieter way to end the day
Don't: You won't BELIEVE this sleep hack

Error / empty state
Do: Nothing here yet. That is fine.
Don't: Oops! Let's get you back on track.

Social
Do: The lamp is dimmable for a reason.
Don't: Who else is prioritizing their rest era?

## Humor
Light and rare. Never sarcastic toward the customer. Never meme-voice.
