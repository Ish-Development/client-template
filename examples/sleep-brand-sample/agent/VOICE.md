## Syntax and rhythm
Prefer short lines. One claim at a time. Let white space do work.

## We say
"Put the phone in another room."
"This is magnesium glycinate. It is the form that sits easiest for most people."
"If you are still awake, that is allowed."

## We do not say
"Unlock your best self."
"Optimize your circadian stack."
"Join the sleep revolution."
"Wellness journey."

## Do and don't, by channel

Website headline
Do: Sleep that does not ask more of you.
Don't: The future of restorative performance.

Product description
Do: A linen eye mask weighted just enough to stay put. No scent. No lights.
Don't: A luxurious slumber essential designed to elevate your nightly ritual.

Email subject
Do: A quieter way to end the day
Don't: You won't BELIEVE this sleep hack

Error / empty state
Do: Nothing here yet. That is fine.
Don't: Oops! Let's get you back on track.

Social
Do: The lamp is dimmable for a reason.
Don't: Who else is prioritizing their rest era?

## Humor
Light and rare. Never sarcastic toward the customer. Never meme-voice.
