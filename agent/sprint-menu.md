# Sprint menu

Studio method. One card is one sprint, and one sprint gives one result.
Clients pick from this menu every quarter. Names are drafts.
The growth review suggests sprints from this menu.

| Sprint | Result | We make | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collect | People who never heard of them start finding the site, and leave an email. | 3-4 guides people find on Google. Each one asks for an email. | Collector |
| Campaign | A launch or a campaign gets its own front door. | One landing page for the launch or the season. | Attractor |
| Showcase | One part of the site people remember. | One special piece with motion, placed where the business will not change. | The memorable one |
| Decision | A buyer can make the hard choice without booking a call. | A price table, a comparison, or a spec sheet, built from their own numbers. | Informer / Converter |
| Convert | The site stops losing people it already won. | Fixes based on the data since launch, wherever visitors drop off. | Converter |
| Review | A short list of what to build next quarter, with proof. | Our audit, run again on their own live site. | All four |

When we invent a new sprint, we add a row.
